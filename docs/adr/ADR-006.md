# ADR-006: Kafka event semantics

Status: Accepted technical baseline under autonomous task authority, 2026-10-08. Not external approval.

## Context

FR-007,010,020,022,032; NFR-005,011. Greenfield repository; workload targets are assumptions.

## Decision

At least once, per-key partition order, versioned Avro BACKWARD_TRANSITIVE.

## Alternatives

Exactly once DB+Kafka claim; global order.

## Rationale

Duplicates/replays are unavoidable at DB/broker boundary.

## Consequences

Inbox/version guards and replay limits; typed schemas before business producers.

## Operational Impact

DLQ owner, lag/outbox metrics and schema registration CI.
